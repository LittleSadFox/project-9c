# Errors — 2026-10-22

## 1. Mac sync_volume multipart SignatureDoesNotMatch

- Time (UTC): 2026-10-22
- Where: Mac / volume S3
- Symptom: Upload of a ~40MiB golden video failed with `CreateMultipartUpload` `SignatureDoesNotMatch`.
- Cause: AWS CLI `s3 cp` starts multipart above 8MB. Runpod S3 rejects that path. A single `PutObject` works.
- Next action: Use `aws s3api put-object` only. Do not change `AWS_CONFIG_FILE`. Do not write `~/.aws/config`. Refuse files over 150MiB before upload. Multipart is not supported yet.

## 3. Webhook ping EXIT trap terminated the pod twice

- Time (UTC): 2026-10-22
- Where: review of PR #15
- Symptom: `cc_curl_post_json` re-installed `trap cleanup EXIT` inside `$(...)`. `cc_terminate_self` ran from the ping subshell before `webhook ping ok`.

## 6. Timeout fallback could kill the caller group

- Time (UTC): 2026-10-22
- Where: review of PR #15
- Symptom: `kill -- -$pid` after `cmd &` could fail, or it could kill `run_job` or the watchdog.
- Cause: Non-interactive bash does not give `cmd &` its own process group. macOS has no `setsid`.
- Next action: Start the child with `perl setpgrp` or `set -m`. Compare pgids before a group-kill. Kill only that pid when the groups match. Assert no stray `sleep` and that the parent still logs.
